## About

City Parkings allows you to see at a glance the number of available parking spaces in your city's parking lots.

Stop wasting time and avoid the unpleasant surprise of finding a full parking lot when you arrive!

## Features

- **Real-time Availability**: Check available parking spots instantly
- **Multiple Cities**: Covers Orléans, Strasbourg, and more
- **Favorites**: Mark your favorite parking spots with a heart
- **Quick Navigation**: One click to navigate to your favorite parking
